Equipment

  • Leave-in probe for the turkey + instant-read thermometer
  • Two 12-inch cast-iron pans plus a grill basket or sheet pan for the sprouts (3 batches)
  • Heatproof gloves, foil, kitchen towels, sheet pans, parchment
  • Pans: two metal 9x13 (potatoes), greased 9x13 + 8x8 (stuffing), oiled 9x13 (focaccia)

Turkey temperatures

Pull: breast 155–157°F, thighs 170–175°F. Carryover takes the breast to 165°F. Rest 30–90 min under foil + towels.

Gluten-free label checks

  • Bacon · white miso (not barley/mugi) · tamari · broth
