Example recipes

Example recipes, all gluten-free as written. Large batches (about 16 servings); scale to your table. Times match the example timeline for a 7:30 PM dinner.

1. Spatchcocked Turkey (Keg)

20-lb turkey, spatchcocked
  1. 2:00 PM Light the Keg. Indirect: deflector in, drip pan under the grate. Stabilize at 325–350°F. Turkey out of the fridge.
  2. 3:00 PM Turkey on, skin side up. Probe in the thickest part of the breast, not touching bone.
  3. Expect about 3 hours (range 2½–3½).
  4. 4:45 PM check: breast under 120°F → open vents, push to 375°F. Over 140°F → close vents slightly, drift toward 300°F. In between → leave it.
  5. Pull when breast reads 155–157°F and thighs 170–175°F (window 5:30–6:40; must be off by 6:40). Carryover brings the breast to 165°F.
  6. Tent with foil, drape with towels. Rest 30–90 min.
  7. 7:00 PM Carve (~15 min). On the platter by 7:15.

2. Gluten-Free Scalloped Potatoes (coconut milk)

Assembled last night: two metal 9x13 pans in the fridge.

Baking today

  1. 4:45 PM Out of the fridge.
  2. 5:30 PM Oven 1 at 375°F, upper rack, covered with foil.
  3. 6:35 PM Uncover. Optional light dusting of nanami togarashi.
  4. 7:15 PM Out when bubbling, golden, and a knife slides easily through the center. If firm, re-cover and bake 10–15 min more.
  5. Rest 15 min. Chopped chives just before serving.

Reference recipe

  • 8 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, sliced ⅛ in
  • 4 cans (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 cups GF broth, cold
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 8 garlic cloves, minced
  • 12 oz Gruyère + 12 oz sharp cheddar (1½ cups reserved for topping)
  • 4 tsp kosher salt, 1½ tsp pepper, ½ tsp nutmeg, 2 Tbsp fresh thyme
  • ¼ cup chopped chives, to garnish

Sauce: sauté onions in butter, add garlic. Whisk cornstarch into cold broth. Add coconut milk, slurry and seasonings; simmer until slightly thickened. Layer potatoes, sauce and cheese in thirds in each pan. Top with reserved cheese.

3. GF Mushroom & Herb Stuffing with Apples

Bakes in one 9x13 + one 8x8
  • 750 g GF bread, crust on, ¾-in cubes (~15 cups)
  • 9 Tbsp unsalted butter (3 / 3 / 3)
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1200 g mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake caps, oyster/maitake)
  • 1½ Tbsp GF tamari
  • 1½ medium onions, diced
  • 4–5 celery stalks, thinly sliced
  • 1½ tsp salt, ¾ tsp pepper
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
  • ¾ tsp smoked paprika
  • Generous ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1½ tsp dried thyme
  • 1½ Tbsp fresh sage + ¾ tsp dried (or 2¼ tsp dried)
  • 3 medium apples, diced (~360 g)
  • 6 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 2⅔ cups (630 ml) low-sodium GF broth
  • 3 large eggs
  • Optional: 1 cup (120 g) dried cranberries
  1. Dry the bread (12:35). Oven 2 at 300°F, three sheet pans, 30–40 min, stir once, until hard all the way through. Cool on the pans.
  2. Brown the mushrooms (3:10). Chop cremini small, slice shiitake caps, tear oyster/maitake. Oil + 3 Tbsp butter, medium-high. Three batches, 8–10 min each, until deep brown. Stir in tamari. To a bowl.
  3. Aromatics. 3 Tbsp butter in the same skillet. Onion, celery, salt, pepper: 4 min. Add garlic, fennel, paprika, pepper flakes, thyme, sage: 1 min.
  4. Apples. 3–4 min until barely softened. Return mushrooms. Off heat, stir in parsley. Should taste slightly over-seasoned. Cool on the counter until 5:45 (~4:05).
  5. Combine (5:45). Bread + mushroom mix + cranberries in a big bowl. Whisk eggs into broth; pour over ¾ of it, fold gently. Wait 5 min; add the rest only if dry cubes remain. Into greased 9x13 + 8x8. Dot with the last 3 Tbsp butter.
  6. Bake (Oven 1, 375°F). Covered 6:15–6:50, lower rack. Uncover; out at 7:15 when browned and crisp. The 8x8 may finish a few minutes early.

4. Miso Maple Butter Roasted Squash (cubed)

3 butternut + 1 acorn squash · 1-inch cubes · 2 to 3 sheet pans, single layer

Squash

  • 3 butternut squash, peeled and seeded
  • 1 acorn squash, seeded, skin on
  • 4 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp pepper

Glaze

  • 5 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 4 Tbsp white miso (GF, not barley/mugi)
  • 4 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated

Garnish

  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 3 scallions, thinly sliced
  • Pinch nanami togarashi (optional)
  1. Prep (2:15). Peel the butternut, cut off the neck, halve the bulb and scoop the seeds. Halve and seed the acorn (skin stays on). Cut everything into 1-inch cubes. Keep in bags in the fridge until 6:15.
  2. Whisk the glaze (rewarm briefly before using).
  3. 5:40 Toss the cubes with oil, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer on parchment-lined sheet pans (about 2 lb of cubes per pan; crowding steams instead of browning).
  4. 5:40 Preheat oven 2 to 425°F. 6:00 pans in on the upper and lower racks.
  5. 6:20 Stir the cubes and swap the pans top to bottom.
  6. 6:25 Toss with half the glaze and back in. ~10 min more until tender and lacquered. Watch: glaze scorches.
  7. 6:35 Out, foil loosely. 7:15 back in (with the focaccia) for 5 min to rewarm.
  8. 7:20 Toss with the remaining glaze. Sesame, scallions, togarashi.
Don't have room for three pans in oven 2? Roast two full pans and keep the extra cubes for tomorrow rather than crowding.

5. Bacon

Half into the Brussels sprouts, half on the side · 1½ lb thick-cut (check GF label)
  1. 1:35 Oven 1 at 400°F. Bacon on parchment-lined sheet pans (or racks in pans).
  2. 18–25 min until crisp (check 1:50, out ~1:58). Drain on paper towels.
  3. Save the rendered fat: about 3 Tbsp needed for the sprouts.
  4. Chop bite-size. Half into the bacon-fat sprouts (Keg 3 lb, oven 1 lb), half for a side dish. The plain sprouts get no bacon.
  5. Room temp is fine. Optionally rewarm the side portion 5 min in oven 2 at 7:15 with the squash.

8. Glazed Ham (precooked, 3.6 kg / ~8 lb)

Oven 1 · reheating only · target 140°F in the center · check the ham label for gluten
  1. 3:15 Oven 1 at 325°F. Ham cut side down in a roasting pan with a splash of water (about ½ cup). Cover tightly with foil.
  2. Heat about 10–12 min per lb: roughly 75 min covered.
  3. 4:30 Uncover. Score the surface in a diamond pattern, stud a whole clove at each intersection. Brush on half the glaze. Oven 1 → 375°F.
  4. 4:40 Brush on the rest of the glaze.
  5. 4:50 Out when the center reads 140°F. Tent with foil, rest 20 min, then slice.
  6. Oven 1 preheats to 375°F at 5:00 for the potatoes (5:30) as before.
  7. Holding: it's already cooked, so served warm or room temp is fine. Keep sliced ham covered and don't leave it out more than about 2 h; if it will sit longer, refrigerate and rewarm covered 10 min in oven 1 at 7:15.

Simple glaze (use what you have; check labels for GF)

  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard (check GF)
  • 2 Tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • ¼ cup orange or pineapple juice
  • Whole cloves for studding
Stir the glaze in a small saucepan over low heat 2–3 min until it brushes easily.

10. Caesar Salad

Big wooden salad bowl · 4 of the 6 romaine hearts (keep 2 spare) · dress late so it stays crisp

Ingredients

  • 4 romaine hearts (about 2 lb); the other 2 are spare if the bowl looks light
  • ¾ to 1 cup olive oil
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 1 Tbsp freshly ground black pepper (a whole bunch of grinding)
  • Juice of 1½ lemons (about ¼ cup)
  • 3 Tbsp Dijon mustard (check label for GF)
  • 1 Tbsp gluten-free Worcestershire
  • 6 Tbsp Hellmann's mayo (check label for GF)
  • 6 oz block of Parmesan, about 1½ cups grated

Steps (the announcer calls each time)

  1. 5:20 Lettuce and cheese. Cut the romaine hearts in 1-inch pieces, wash, and dry completely (salad spinner, then towels). Pile in a bowl or bag lined with a dry paper towel, cover, and refrigerate. Grate the Parmesan and refrigerate it too.
  2. 6:05 Dressing base, no mayo. In the bottom of the big wooden bowl: olive oil, garlic squished or minced, pepper, lemon juice, Dijon, Worcestershire. Whisk. Taste: it should be sharp and peppery. Leave it at the bottom, no lettuce yet.
  3. 7:16 Mayo. Whisk the 6 Tbsp mayo into the dressing until creamy.
  4. 7:22 Toss. Lettuce into the bowl, toss until coated, then Parmesan over the top and toss again. Taste, add a pinch of salt or more pepper only if needed (Parmesan is salty). To the table with everything else at 7:25.
Don't dress or toss the lettuce before 7:22. Lettuce goes soggy; the dressing base can sit for an hour. No croutons in this recipe, so nothing to worry about for gluten except the Dijon, Worcestershire and mayo labels.

11. Cranberry Sauce (gluten-free)

Per 12 oz bag. Double for 2 bags. Start 5:00 PM, off the heat 5:17 PM.
  • 12 oz fresh cranberries
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ¾ cup water
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt
  1. Everything into a saucepan. Medium heat, stir until it boils.
  2. Simmer 10–12 min, stirring often. The berries pop and the sauce thickens.
  3. Taste. Add a spoonful more sugar if too tart.
  4. Off the heat, into a bowl. Cool on the counter, then fridge. It thickens as it cools.

12. Turkey Gravy (gluten-free, cornstarch)

Makes about 4 cups. Start the stock 6:40 PM, gravy 7:05 PM, on the table by 7:30.
  • Turkey drippings (150–200 ml) from the pan
  • Neck and any trimmings
  • 2 carrots, 2 celery stalks, 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 Tbsp oil or margarine for browning
  • About 6 cups water (or half water, half GF chicken broth, check label)
  • 2 Tbsp margarine
  • 3 Tbsp cornstarch + 3 Tbsp cold water
  • Salt, pepper, thyme if you have it, splash of vinegar or wine (optional)
  1. 6:40 Stock. Medium-high heat in a big pot, oil in. Brown the neck and trimmings 5 min. Add carrot, celery, onion, brown 3 min more.
  2. Pour a little water into the turkey roasting pan, scrape up the browned bits, and add all of it to the pot. Add the rest of the water (6 cups total).
  3. Bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered about 30 min. Don't salt yet. It should reduce to about 4½ cups.
  4. 7:05 Gravy. Strain the stock into a bowl (press the veg). Spoon the fat off the drippings and keep them with the stock. You want about 4 cups.
  5. Back in the pot: melt the margarine, add the stock and drippings, bring to a simmer.
  6. Stir the cornstarch with the cold water until smooth. Pour into the simmering stock while whisking. Simmer 2 min until it coats a spoon. It thickens more as it cools.
  7. Taste. Salt and pepper, a pinch of thyme, a splash of vinegar if it's flat. Too thick? Add a splash of water.
  8. Keep warm on the lowest heat, whisk before serving. Pour into a jug.
Gluten-free check: no flour, no soy sauce, no bouillon cubes unless the label says gluten-free. Cornstarch is fine.
